Understanding Hair Growth Stages

Why regular appointments are essential for maximum hair destruction

Anagen Phase

Active Growth

The hair is actively growing and connected to the papilla (blood supply). This is the ONLY stage where electrolysis can permanently destroy the follicle. The hair has maximum pigment and is firmly rooted.

Catagen Phase

Transition

The hair detaches from the papilla and begins to shrink. The follicle shortens and the hair stops growing. Treatment during this phase is less effective as the target cells are separating.

Telogen Phase

Resting

The hair is dormant and will eventually shed. The follicle is inactive and cannot be effectively treated. This is why some hairs appear to "come back" — they were in a resting phase during previous treatments.

📅

Why Regular Appointments Are Critical

At any given time, only 20-30% of your hair follicles are in the anagen (active growth) phase — the only phase where permanent destruction is possible. Hair cycles through these stages at different rates, which means:

  • Regular appointments catch each hair in its vulnerable anagen phase
  • Consistent treatment schedules ensure maximum follicle destruction
  • Skipping appointments allows hairs to cycle back without being treated
  • Most areas require treatments every 1-2 weeks initially for best results

Commitment to your treatment schedule is the key to achieving permanent results in the shortest possible time.
